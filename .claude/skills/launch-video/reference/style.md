# The style, measured

From the reference launch video (Pulse, 52 s, 1920x1080, 30 fps) and the Instagram "made with one skill" ad.

## Type
- One grotesk, medium weight (Inter Tight 500 here), tight tracking (-0.045em). ~100 px at 1080p for a caption line,
  240–300 px for the hero word, 150 px for the wordmark, 44–60 px for taglines and URLs.
- Off-white `#ECEDF1` on black, near-black `#0B0B0C` on `#FDFDFD`. One accent (`#0DDD4B` green in the reference).
- Pills: accent fill, white semibold text at 0.9 em, radius ~0.16 em, padding ~0.2 em. One per line.

## Motion
- Words reveal as they're spoken: 0.34 s, blur 14 px → 0, rise 0.22 em, opacity in. They start 0.06 s before the word.
- A centred line re-centres smoothly as each word takes its space (the signature move). Pills grow with their words.
- Counters count up over ~0.9 s with ease-out.
- Typed lines: caret, letters appear across the spoken word, the newest letters flash accent.
- Text leaves with a 0.24 s blur-out; UI scenes hard-cut.
- UI windows enter with a short rise + blur (0.6 s), then push in slowly (1.00 → 1.07) or tilt up in 3D.
- Cursor moves ease in-out between keys; clicks dip the cursor and ring the accent.
- Reveal: riser into a huge glowing word, boom on it, then the orb pops with rings and slides into the lockup.

## Pacing
- One line per scene, ~2.6 spoken words per second, a new visual every 1.5–4 s.
- Voice starts at 0. The music bed is present under the voice the whole time (about 2–3 dB below the voice in the
  gaps), with the beat dropping at the reveal.
- Backgrounds: dark (hook, reveal) → light (product UI) → dark (lockup, CTA).

## What makes it work
- Every feature line is shown in the real UI at the moment it's said, with the cursor or a focus lift pointing at it.
- Short lines and fragments: "Track your leads." "Own a content agency?" "And yours too."
- Concrete numbers in the hook (real ones).

# App ad style, measured

From a fomo spec ad (Sevas, 19.7 s, 1920x1080, music only, made with After Effects + Blender).

- Palette: navy-black `#04050d` text cards with a soft accent glow in the middle; device shots on a pale accent wash
  (accent tint at the top fading to white) or in the dark. White type on dark, near-black on the wash.
- Type: one clean grotesk at regular weight (400) for the lines, ~100 px; one bold all-caps word (~190 px, 800) on a
  light beam rising from the bottom edge. The wordmark alone at the end.
- Rhythm: text card (one short line, word by word, ~0.9–1.1 s) → device shot (~1.3–1.8 s) → text card..., cuts on the
  beat, a burst of sparkles around one feature. No voiceover; the music carries it.
- Camera: the device is never still and never flat: it rises out of the dark, orbits slowly, dips into a low-angle
  close-up of one control, or flies over steeply with lens blur that pulls into focus.
- Lines name the feature on screen: "One vision." "One action a day." "A coach that listens." "With your people."
